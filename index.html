The house on Nowshera Road

Built around one idea: the platter comes first.

Platter House is the Mardan stop for a proper mixed BBQ — meats marinated, grilled over coal and served hot on a burner at your table, with rice and bread to go around. It is the kind of place families plan an evening around: private Arab-style cabins, traditional floor seating, and outdoor seating on charpais when the weather is kind.

Regulars come back for the same reasons the listings keep repeating — generous portions, juicy, well-marinated meat, and service that keeps a big table moving. Order one full platter for the table, add a biryani, and let the coal burner do the talking.

  • Served on coal burners — platters stay hot and sizzling at the table.
  • Private cabins & floor seating — Arab-style cabins alongside traditional seating.
  • Outdoor seating on charpais — open-air dining in the evening.
  • Family-friendly — built for groups, kids and big appetites.

The Coal Fire

It starts with the coals.

The grill runs on charcoal, and the platter runs on the burner. Coals are lit, meats are marinated and grilled to order, and the platter is carried to the table still sizzling — that is the whole method, and the reason the food arrives hot.

Good to Know

Before you come.

Answers to the questions people ask most about Platter House — all drawn from the restaurant’s public listings.

What is Platter House known for?

Mixed BBQ platters served on coal burners, along with chicken biryani and Platter House special rice. The full platter BBQ with rice is the house signature.

Where is the restaurant?

Pull, Nowshera Road near Ring Road, Behram Khan Kilay, Mardan 23200 — the main branch on Nowshera Road.

What are the opening hours?

Open every day, 9:00 AM – 11:00 PM, including weekends.

Is it good for families and groups?

Yes — the restaurant is listed as family-friendly, with Arab-style private cabins, traditional floor seating and outdoor seating on charpais.

Do I need to book ahead?

Walk-ins are welcome, but for a large group or a private cabin it is best to call ahead on 0334 8998855.

How do I find it online?

The restaurant is on TikTok as @platterhousemardan. For orders and bookings, call 0334 8998855.
